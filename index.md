Analysis of Sentinel-2 satellite imagery in QGIS: vegetation mapping with NDVI and flood mapping with false color composites and NDWI.

This module was taught as part of the Purdue-NSF GIS Cybertraining for the Social Sciences Workshop, a two-day workshop held at Purdue University in 2026 (Day 1, Session A QGIS track, part 3). See the [Workshop 2026 page](https://spatialturn.github.io/workshop2026.html) for the full program.

It is part of [Enabling the Geospatial Turn in the Social Sciences](https://spatialturn.github.io/), a set of training modules developed at Purdue University with support from the National Science Foundation.
